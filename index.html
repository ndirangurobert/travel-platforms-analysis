03 / Platform profiles

Four strategic positions to understand.

These profiles summarise each platform's broad proposition and the execution questions that matter most.

Booking.com

PROFILE / 01

An accommodation-first marketplace extending into flights, transport and attractions.

Where it is strong

  • Broad accommodation choice across destinations and property types.
  • Search, reviews and conversion tools built around stay selection.
  • Opportunities to connect more parts of the trip in one place.

Watch-outs

  • Keep total pricing and cancellation terms easy to understand.
  • Maintain availability and partner quality across a very broad supply base.
Strategic test: Can it turn accommodation intent into a trusted end-to-end trip?

Expedia

PROFILE / 02

A broad travel marketplace with a strong proposition around trip components and package value.

Where it is strong

  • Hotels, flights, cars, cruises, activities and package options.
  • Cross-category trip planning and bundling opportunities.
  • One Key loyalty proposition in selected markets.

Watch-outs

  • Make brand architecture and loyalty benefits simple to navigate.
  • Show package savings and final trip costs transparently.
Strategic test: Does bundling create a noticeably easier or better-value trip?

Airbnb

PROFILE / 03

A distinctive-stay proposition built around homes, hosts and a more local-feeling experience.

Where it is strong

  • Homes and unusual accommodation beyond conventional hotels.
  • Host community, guest reviews and destination-led discovery.
  • Experiences and services that can extend the stay proposition.

Watch-outs

  • Improve consistency in quality, fees and cancellation expectations.
  • Navigate local regulation and community concerns.
Strategic test: Can it make total cost and quality more predictable without losing uniqueness?

Trip.com

PROFILE / 04

A broad travel ecosystem combining accommodation, transport ticketing and travel services.

Where it is strong

  • Accommodation, transport, packaged tours and corporate travel.
  • A portfolio that includes Trip.com, Ctrip, Qunar and Skyscanner.
  • Strong relevance in Asian travel markets and cross-border journeys.

Watch-outs

  • Build global trust through localisation and dependable service.
  • Keep support quality consistent across markets and products.
Strategic test: Can ecosystem breadth translate into a simple, trusted global experience?

05 / Strategic synthesis

The next advantage is confidence, not just choice.

More listings and more trip components can attract attention. Trustworthy execution is what makes travellers comfortable completing the journey.

01

Transparent total pricing

Make fees, taxes, cancellation rules and package savings understandable before checkout.

02

Own more of the journey

Connect discovery, booking, changes and in-trip support into one coherent experience.

03

Personalise without friction

Use relevant context to simplify choices, not overwhelm travellers with more decisions.

04

Build trust into the product

Make quality signals, service standards and support options visible at decision time.

06 / What to watch next

Four tests for the next phase of travel competition.

The strategic opportunity is to turn broad inventory into a dependable, connected and distinctly useful travel experience.

Value clarity

Can travellers see the real cost and terms early?

Journey ownership

Who supports the traveller when plans change?

Useful personalisation

Does relevance make decisions easier?

Reliable supply

Can quality stay consistent as choice expands?
